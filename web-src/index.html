How training works

Every prepared move of yours has one of five states. The queue always drills the most urgent first:

WeakMissed more often than answered — these always come first.
DueSpaced repetition says it's time to review this move now.
NewNever trained. Taught first (arrow + idea), then tested later.
LearningAnswered before, still being spaced out.
MasteredRecalled correctly 3+ times with no recent misses — reviews now arrive days apart. (“Polish” cards keep this material warm.)

“In a row” is this session only: consecutive first-try correct answers.
Day streak 🔥 counts calendar days (your local time) with at least one graded training move — one card a day keeps it alive.
