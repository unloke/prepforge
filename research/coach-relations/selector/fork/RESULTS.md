## Rejected refinement

captures) changed 17 pool cards; sol preferred the first rule on 15, the refinement on 1, both bad
1 (fork/r2, fork-r2-key.jsonl). It misses counter-captures (Qxf4 answered by Qxd7) and gains that
land at the end of a longer run (f4 ... Bxe5 Rxe5 fxe5). Reverted; the shipped rule is the one
judged above.

forker's moves or through promotion (e7 forking both rooks), and a target won later because it is
