# Re-review of the 38 saved studies

This is a sampled move-content review, not a claim that every variation was
strategically verified. `review-content.py` replays every raw tree legally and
selects the first nonempty, most branching and shortest nonempty chapter in each
study (overlaps collapse). The paths, first forks, endpoints and chapter numbers
are saved in [content-review-samples.json](content-review-samples.json). Chapter
names locate evidence; no names, prose, annotations, likes or engine scores enter
the production calculation. The earlier six deeper inspections remain in
[content-review-examples.md](content-review-examples.md).

## Iterations and rejected assumptions

1. The old fixed 16-ply single-policy reference produced narrow 100s and discarded
   chapter plans. Select preferred tiers per occurrence and preserve tied chapter
   plans. Count distinct answered opponent alternatives, not own choices or copies.
2. That still made short instructional side lines dilute long core plans. Use
   preferred opponent tiers for core depth, while all eligible opponent responses
   can establish answered breadth. This is a structural convention, not semantic
   identification of an explanation. A demonstrative mainline chapter can still
   look like a recommendation; do not claim that ambiguity is solved.
3. Reuse the existing `gamePhase` detector. A raw phase switch is insufficient:
   exchanges can switch it early. Require consecutive non-opening positions,
   two quiet plies and no current check. Opening completion absorbs depth;
   later model-game length earns nothing. A 32-ply bound handles routes for which
   no completion is detected. It remains a heuristic, not a tactical certificate.
4. Scope all measurements to their reachable horizon. This fixes out-of-scope
   exposure but **did not fix** the opening-chooser truncation gain: its core
   Replies rose 78.95→82.35 while Depth barely fell. Replies' changing denominator
   double-counted missing answers already represented by Depth. Keep Replies as
   an actionable diagnostic, use sqrt(Reach × Depth) for the total, and do not
   credit an opponent move whose required own response is absent. This addresses
   the cause rather than fitting a weight to that source's title or likes.
5. Repeated positions with different preferred replies and similar structures
   with different plans describe choices and learning load. They do not establish
   poor preparation. Keep Consistency and Ease in the five-axis profile, outside
   the total. Require comparable complete own setups/material/king phase for Ease;
   compare two-action plans so swapping their order need not look different.

The [calibration report](ranking-calibration.json) records controlled shortening,
response deletion and parameter sensitivity on all 45 side reports. The frozen
[baseline](calibration-baselines.json) retains the failed first phase trial.
All 38 informed development; none is an untouched validation holdout. High likes
are positive reception context, never a low-like negative label. Results support
preparation structure, not a universal numeric ranking of teaching quality.
