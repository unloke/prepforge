# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individual chess players and coaches preparing openings, reviewing games, training positions, and studying opponents. Team collaboration supports their work but is not the primary desktop workflow.

## Product Purpose

PrepForge Chess connects game analysis, repertoire building, training, opponent scouting, and team sharing in one working environment. Success means users can move from evidence in games to an opening plan and practice it efficiently.

## Operating Context

Users work on a chessboard, inspect moves and engine feedback, edit PGN and repertoires, review recent games, study opponent routes, and share repertoires with teams. The desktop interface is an information-dense professional tool.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The desktop redesign must preserve behavior, accessibility, keyboard controls, responsive mobile layouts, E2E hooks, and stable layout during async updates. Scout ranking, backend, database schema, engine lifecycle, and product logic are outside scope.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the PrepForge name, dark theme, and warm amber visual language. Chessboards are the primary operating surface in Analyze, Build, and Train.

## Product Principles

- Keep the board large and immediately usable.
- Put frequent actions before secondary information.
- Show dense information with clear hierarchy and stable regions.
- Make evidence and next actions easy to connect across views.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, keyboard operation, and responsive access on mobile.
